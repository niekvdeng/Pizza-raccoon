Mapa

Ten real artefacts, each with its own museum label. One raccoon, acting entirely without a mandate.

Pizza Raccoon

A firm called Patrimonio S.A. has crated up ten of Mexico's treasures and posted uniformed men over every one. You are a raccoon. You have a pizza cutter, an appetite, and absolutely no legal standing.

Press E to lift an artefact, space to swing. You can carry three — four is physically possible but undignified — and nothing counts until you've run it back to the casa segura on the plaza. The more you're carrying, the harder they look.

There is a stray dog near the market. Press E beside it to share a slice, and it will follow you around barking at uniforms. Loyalty lasts about eighty seconds and costs one slice to renew.
